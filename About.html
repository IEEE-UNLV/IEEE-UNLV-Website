About Us

Who We Are

IEEE UNLV is the student branch of the world's largest technical professional organization, bringing together students in electrical engineering, electronics, communications, computer engineering, computer science, and related fields.

What We Do

We provide opportunities for students to learn, grow, and connect with the broader engineering community through events, workshops, and professional development activities.

What We Do

Share Knowledge

We host talks, discussions, and events covering emerging technology, research, and industry trends.

Build Community

We connect students with faculty, professionals, and each other to grow both technically and professionally.

Uphold the Profession

As part of IEEE, we promote ethical conduct and collaborate with other organizations to advance engineering for the public good.

Support Learning

We work to enhance the educational experience at UNLV and help members understand technology's impact on society.
